Features

Built for clans

/profile

Player profiles

WARDOG level, all six role levels, cash and worth. Show More adds XP progress to the next level in every role, global rank and recent gains.

Always fresh

Stats sync themselves

Active players are refreshed every 30 minutes, with nothing to paste again. Members are told when their account needs refreshing.

/leaderboard

Leaderboards

Rank your clan by level, total XP, worth, cash, this week's gains, or any role from Assault to Pilot.

/compare

Head to head

Put two members side by side: who leads, where each one is ahead, and both profiles in full.

Announcements

Recaps and promotions

A weekly recap of the top climbers, a season recap when a season ends, and shout-outs for promotions and milestone levels.

Per server

Your clan, your rules

Each server has its own ranks, channels and settings. Someone in two clans links once and shows up in both.

Setup

Ready in two minutes

  1. Add the bot

    Press Add to Discord and pick your server. It posts a short welcome with the setup steps.

  2. Choose a channel

    Run /admin announce-channel for promotions and recaps. Add /setup-ranks if you want E–S rank roles.

  3. Members link up

    Each member links their WARDOGS account on the WARDOGS Database, then presses I've linked my account in Discord. Nothing to copy or paste.

FAQ

Questions

Where do the stats come from?

From the WARDOGS Database (wardogs.tools), through its API, used with its owner's permission. Each member links their own WARDOGS account there; the bot never sees anyone who hasn't.

Does the bot read our messages?

No. It only responds to its own slash commands and buttons, and doesn't request access to message content at all.

How do I remove my data?

Run /unlink in any server that uses the bot. Your link and stats are deleted everywhere straight away. The privacy policy has the details.

Is this an official WARDOGS bot?

No. Wardog Assistant is a fan-made project and isn't affiliated with Bulkhead or Team17. Player data comes from the WARDOGS Database, with its owner's permission.
